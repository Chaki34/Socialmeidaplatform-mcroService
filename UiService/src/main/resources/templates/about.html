Clear answers to common questions about our platform and support.

What is Flexus Global Fulfillment?
Flexus combines real-time networking with smart orchestration to deliver professional projects through the best talent for faster and more reliable results globally.
How do I switch between accounts?
On the Main Menu, you will find a "Profile Switch" button that allows you to swap between your Personal, Professional, and Enterprise identities instantly.
Is the Flexus Pulse Dynamic Safety Stock active?
Yes, our Pulse AI autonomously adjusts your professional resource allocation based on real-time market demand and industry shifts.
What are the Customer Success offerings?
We offer a comprehensive suite of advisory, technical, and concierge services designed to ensure your organization hits its ROI targets within 6 months.
Does Flexus support multi-currency payroll?
Absolutely. We handle cross-border payments in over 80 currencies, ensuring compliance with local tax laws in every country we operate in.
How secure is my proprietary project data?
We utilize AES-256 bank-grade encryption. Your data is your own; Flexus never sells or uses your project details for training external AI models.

Yes, Flexus features a robust API and native integrations for Salesforce, HubSpot, and Microsoft Dynamics 365.

This is our gold-standard vetting process that verifies identity, past project success, and skill proficiency through a peer-reviewed ledger.
